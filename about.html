Bridging the Gap Between
Abundance and Scarcity

I am on a mission to eliminate food waste while ensuring that no one goes to bed hungry. Technology meets empathy.

I developed this Waste Food Management and Donation platform to seamlessly connect surplus food from any place, event, or home party with NGOs and people in need. By optimizing distribution logistics, my goal is to drastically reduce the food waste problem and make a positive impact in our community.
